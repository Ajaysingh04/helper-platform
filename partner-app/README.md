# 🛠️ Helper Partner Mobile App (Vendor & Specialist Portal)

Dedicated standalone mobile application for technicians, plumbers, electricians, AC mechanics, home cleaners, beauticians, and contractors on the Helper Platform.

---

## 📱 Key Features

1. **Duty Switch (Online / Offline Toggle)**:
   - Real-time online radar scanning in Indore city (10km radius).
   - Turn offline whenever on a break or after working hours.

2. **Incoming Job Radar (45s Countdown Alert)**:
   - Real-time incoming lead with loud audio notification.
   - Shows customer name, exact address, estimated distance, job checklist and payout amount.
   - Accept or Pass buttons.

3. **Active Work Order Execution**:
   - One-tap customer call & Google Maps navigation.
   - **4-Digit Customer Start OTP**: Protects customer and technician by ensuring work starts only after secure on-site verification.
   - Check off service milestones.

4. **Instant Payment & UPI QR Generator**:
   - Customer can scan on-screen UPI QR code with GPay/PhonePe/Paytm or pay cash.

5. **Daily Earnings & Instant Withdrawal**:
   - Real-time wallet tracking (Today, This Week, This Month).
   - Direct instant payout to any UPI ID / Bank account.

---

## 🚀 Running & Building

- **Install on Device**:
  ```bash
  cd mobile/android
  gradlew installPartnerDebug
  ```
- **Package Name**: `com.helper.partner`
- **Display Name on Phone**: `Helper Partner`
